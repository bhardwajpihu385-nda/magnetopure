In many rural areas, people rely on traditional sources like village pumps where water often contains invisible microplastics and harmful contaminants, leading to severe health issues.

The MagneToPure Solution

By implementing our low-cost, zero-electricity magnetic filtration system, communities can easily transform contaminated water into safe, crystal-clear drinking water.

Our Innovation & Prototype Mechanism

Zero-Electricity Gravity-Fed Magnetic Microplastic Filter

How MagneToPure Works

Our breakthrough household water-filter concept captures invisible microplastic particles efficiently through an advanced magnetic approach:

  • Gravity-Fed Flow: Water flows naturally through the system using gravity.
  • Eco-Oil & Iron Powder: Special mixture used to bind microscopic plastic particles effectively.
  • External Magnetic Separation: External magnets separate particle sludge safely.
  • Zero Moving Parts: Maintenance-free design with zero electricity costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about MagneToPure? We have answers.

Q: Does MagneToPure require electricity?

A: No! MagneToPure is a 100% zero-electricity, gravity-fed filtration system designed for absolute convenience and eco-friendly usage.

Q: How does it remove microplastics?

A: It uses a special mixture of eco-oil and iron powder to bind microplastic particles, which are then separated safely using external magnets without traditional mesh clogging.

Q: What is the price of the MagneToPure home unit?

A: The proposed selling price is ₹899, keeping it highly affordable for rural and urban households alike, with a low manufacturing cost of ₹450.
